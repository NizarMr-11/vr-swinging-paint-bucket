=== 1. FinalizeKernel formulas (V4PbfSolver.compute) ===
Neighbor loop over predicted-position grid (radius h):
  w = V4Poly6(r, h)
  xsph += (vj - vel) * w
  cohesion -= (diff/r) * w   [diff = predicted_i - predicted_j]
selfW = max(V4Poly6(0, h), 1e-6)   // fixed kernel at r=0, NOT neighbor-weight sum
vel += profile.viscosity * (xsph / selfW)
vel += profile.cohesion * (cohesion / selfW) * _DeltaTime
Notes:
  - Neither term divides by local density or neighbor count.
  - Normalization is ONLY by selfW (constant per particle, ~Poly6(0,h)).
  - No pbfEpsilon-style safeguard on cohesion/xsph denominators.
  - At low neighbor count, numerators shrink but divisor stays fixed -> smaller deltas, not blow-up.
Default profile: cohesion=0.5 viscosity=0.25 restDensity(target)=121173.0
